Hi, I'm Bob. I believe good engineering is restrained — remove everything unnecessary, and what remains stands on its own. This site was built on that idea.

I care more about shipping than demos: quota fuses, retries, human review gates, regression tests — the unglamorous parts that keep AI systems running reliably in the real world often matter more than the model itself.

I'm Bob, a software engineer based in New Zealand. I spent five years in IT operations — running Linux servers, repairing hardware to component level, and closing 1,800+ support cases — before completing a Master's in Applied Computing, specialising in software and database development, at Lincoln University.

Now I design and ship AI products end to end, from idea to production. The parts I care about most are the unglamorous ones: retries, quota fuses, human review gates, regression tests — whatever it takes to keep a system running reliably for real users.
